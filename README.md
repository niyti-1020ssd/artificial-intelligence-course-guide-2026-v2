Understanding Artificial Intelligence: A Brief Overview

Artificial intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems. These processes include learning (the acquisition of information and rules for using it), reasoning (using rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions), and self-correction. As industries strive for greater efficiency and innovation, the demand for professionals skilled in AI is on the rise. But what exactly does an AI course entail?
